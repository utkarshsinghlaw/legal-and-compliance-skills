# Legal and Compliance Skills

A collection of structured AI agent skills designed for Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC).

### Included Skills:
- **aims-audit**: ISO 42001 internal audit readiness.
- **ai-security-guardrails**: 10-point checklist for defending AI systems.
- **uk-compliance-readiness**: Validates business operations against UK GDPR, Companies Act, and CMA guidelines.
- **compliance-readiness**: Generalized cross-jurisdictional regulatory evaluator.
- **legal-workflow-automation**: Automates repetitive legal tasks (intake, NDAs) with strict attorney oversight.
